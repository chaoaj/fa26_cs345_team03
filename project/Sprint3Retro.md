## 2.3 Team Summary
 
This sprint we completed six of our seven backlog items (24 of 29 story points). Combat now works like a real card game: the player is dealt a hand from a shuffled deck, plays cards into the arena, attacks by dragging an arrow to the enemy, and used cards go to the discard pile while new cards are drawn. We also added a working energy system with energy cards, a first boss with its own stats, look, and full deck, and a class selection screen that follows our design. The loading screen was not finished by the end of the sprint. Work on the loading screen was not started during this sprint Next sprint, we want to check in on progress mid-sprint, so items that are falling behind are noticed earlier and the team can help.
